JKL-MVP-001SME Sustainability Score GenerationThe platform shall generate a Sustainability Finance Score for each SME based on submitted business, operational, sustainability (ESG - environmental, carbon-related), readiness and evidence-related data.Functional / Data / ScoringProvides SMEs with a simplified, finance-relevant sustainability performance measure and creates the foundation for matching, benchmarking, improvement recommendations and lender-facing validation.The score must be generated on a standardised scale, such as 0–100. A full score must only be generated when minimum required data fields are completed. Partial scores must be clearly labelled.Company profile, sector, employee size, operational data, sustainability questionnaire responses, environmental data, carbon-related inputs, funding readiness indicators, evidence status.Sustainability Finance Score, score band, scoring status, category-level score outputs, data confidence indicator.User receives a valid Sustainability Finance Score after completing minimum required fields. Score is displayed successfully on the dashboard with relevant category breakdown and status.
7
JKL-MVP-002Weighted Sustainability Finance Scoring CategoriesThe scorecard shall assess SMEs across defined sustainability finance categories, including Environmental, Social, Governance, Business Resilience, Funding Readiness and Data Validation / Data Confidence.Functional/ LogicEnsures the score is structured, explainable and aligned to business improvement, sustainability maturity, evidence quality and funding readiness.Categories must have predefined weightings. Categories must contribute proportionally to the total score. Carbon-related inputs must sit within the Environmental category and must not become a separate standalone MVP module.Questionnaire responses, business activity data, sector mapping, operational indicators, environmental data, evidence status, readiness indicators.Category-level scores, overall weighted Sustainability Finance Score, category labels, data confidence status.Score output includes category breakdown and total Sustainability Finance Score using the agreed weighted category structure.

JKL-MVP-003Score Transparency and Data Confidence ViewThe platform shall show users at a high level how their Sustainability Finance Score was derived, including category-level performance, evidence status and data confidence where available.Functional/UX/ValidationBuilds user trust, improves engagement, reduces black-box scoring concerns and encourages action on weak or unsupported areas.Users must be able to see category-level performance but must not be able to manipulate scoring logic. Evidence status and data confidence must be displayed in simple, non-technical language.Scoring engine output, scoring categories, evidence status, confidence logic, questionnaire completion state.Score explanation, performance summary, data confidence indicator, evidence-backed status.User can view a readable explanation of their score, including category-level performance and evidence confidence where available.

JKL-MVP-004Partial Score HandlingThe platform shall support partial or incomplete scoring where users have not submitted all required information.FunctionalPrevents drop-off and allows early user engagement before full onboarding completion.Partial scores must be clearly labelled. Partial scores must not be treated as final lender-grade outputs.Incomplete onboarding data, questionnaire responsesPartial score, completion warningUser receives partial score and is prompted to complete missing data.

JKL-MVP-005Score Refresh and Recalculation LogicThe platform shall refresh or recalculate the SME Sustainability Finance Score when new relevant data is submitted, including profile data, questionnaire responses, environmental data, evidence uploads, carbon-related inputs or readiness information.Functional/LogicKeeps the score dynamic and reflective of the SME’s latest sustainability, readiness and evidence position.Score must update only when relevant scoring inputs change. Non-scoring edits should not unnecessarily trigger recalculation. Previous score history may be retained where available.Updated company profile, updated questionnaire responses, uploaded evidence, environmental data, carbon-related data, readiness indicators.Updated Sustainability Finance Score, revised category performance, updated evidence confidence and readiness outputs.New score is generated after qualifying data changes are submitted and displayed correctly on the dashboard.

JKL-MVP-021ESG Report GenerationThe platform shall generate a simplified ESG report for SMEs using existing questionnaire responses, sustainability data, environmental inputs, governance information, uploaded evidence and score outputs already captured within the JusKel platform.Functional/ReportingAllows JusKel to create additional value from existing SME data without duplicating onboarding or destabilising the core Sustainability Finance Score.ESG report must reuse existing platform data.

Users must not be required to complete a separate ESG reporting questionnaire in MVP.

Report must clearly show where data is self-declared, evidence-backed or unavailable.

Report must be labelled as platform-generated and not a formal audit or regulatory certification.		SME profile, sustainability questionnaire responses, Environmental/Social/Governance data, carbon-related inputs, evidence status, score outputs.ESG report summary, downloadable ESG report, ESG category narrative, missing data indicators.SME can generate a readable ESG report using existing platform data, with missing or self-declared areas clearly labelled.

Pack must clearly identify unavailable, partial, self-declared and evidence-backed information.

Pack must not claim regulatory approval or audit assurance.

User must be able to review (preview) before download or sharing.		SME profile, ESG responses, carbon inputs, evidence status, data confidence, uploaded documents metadata.Disclosure pack, disclosure summary, missing disclosure items, evidence status summary.SME can generate a structured disclosure pack showing available and missing sustainability information.
