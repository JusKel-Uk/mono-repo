How to validate both SMEs and Lenders at the same time without building lender modules.

We do this in two tracks:
31
Track 1: SME product validation.
Use the actual MVP with SMEs to test:

onboarding completion
data capture
score usefulness
readiness value
funding match relevance
repeat engagement
32
Track 2: Lender design validation

Use lender conversations and mock lender-facing materials to test:

what outputs matter
what data fields they trust
what makes a business “funding ready”
whether your score/readiness outputs are useful inputs
how they would interpret matches and indicative terms

So you are validating both sides, just with different methods.
33
What to show lenders if you do not yet have a lender portal

You do not need a full lender module to get meaningful lender feedback.

You can show them:

SME scorecard output
funding-readiness summary
benchmark snapshot
sample matched funding opportunities
indicative finance terms view
sample SME profile summary
sample recommendation logic
architecture / workflow explanation

In other words:
you show lenders the outputs, not the lender dashboard.

And then you ask:

Would this be useful?
What is missing?
What would you trust?
What would you ignore?
What would make this decision-ready?
What minimum evidence would you need?
How would you want this surfaced?

That gives you extremely valuable validation.
34
What the 3–5 lenders are for right now

They are there to validate things like:

1. Score usefulness - Does the score capture anything meaningful for a funder?
2. Readiness logic - Are you measuring the right readiness indicators?
3. Match logic - Would a lender want SMEs filtered this way?
4. Finance terms logic - Does the indicative logic make sense as a directional signal?
5. Data sufficiency - What extra information would lenders require later?

That is enough for early validation.
